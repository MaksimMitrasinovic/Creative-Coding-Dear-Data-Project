This is my Creative Coding Dear Data project.

I will track my sleeping habits, noting when I go to bed and when I fall asleep.
At 2 data points per day, I will moniter for 8 weeks, bringing my total data points to 112.
I will plot my data as stars on a night sky, with the X-axis representing the days of the week and the Y-axis showing the time.
Data will be extracted out of a 2-D array, allowing anyone to put they're own data in and a version for themselves.
The poits will have some slight X variation to not be displayed in a perfect column.
